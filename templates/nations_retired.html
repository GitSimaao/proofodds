Retired on 9 October 2026

The Nations League page has been retired

From 24 September to 9 October 2026 this page showed model probabilities for UEFA Nations League matches. It has been taken down because the data source behind it is no longer available to us.

Those forecasts were never sealed and never scored. Nothing from this page was written to the ledger, nothing was graded against a closing line, and no figure from it entered the scorecard. There is therefore no record of them to keep, and no result to report. If you saw a number here, it cannot be checked now, and we make no claim about how it did.

Everything this site does seal and score is unchanged: the club matches, the scorecard and the ledger.
