Read the sample size before reading anything else Closing line value converges far faster than profit, and it is still noise for a long time: over a handful of entries, beating or trailing the close says almost nothing. This page exists so that the answer arrives honestly, not so that it arrives quickly. Profit at flat stakes is shown because people ask for it — it is the least informative number here. An Asian handicap whose line moved is settled for P/L but excluded from CLV unless both lines are the same.
