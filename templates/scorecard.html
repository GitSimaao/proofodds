Is a stated 30% really 30%?

{{ calibration_chart|safe }}

Calibration and sharpness are different things, and only one of them is our problem. If the dots sit on the diagonal, a stated probability means what it says — the model is not overconfident, it is simply not seeing as much as the market sees.

A model can be perfectly calibrated and still lose on log loss, by hedging towards the base rates when the market is right to commit. That is what the gap above is mostly made of.

What this page will never do

No ROI headline

Return on investment over a few hundred bets is mostly noise, and it is the number every tipster picks because it is the easiest to flatter. Log loss cannot be cherry-picked.

No hidden window

Every graded match since the first publication is in the numbers above. There is no "since we improved the model" start date.

No quiet edits

The ledger is a hash chain in a public repository. If a past prediction changed, this site would say so itself.
